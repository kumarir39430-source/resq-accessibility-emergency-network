# ResQ pitch kit

## 10 seconds
Emergency apps assume you can unlock, see, type, speak and hear. Many people cannot. ResQ adapts to the person.

## 30 seconds: why we are different
One chain, end to end: adaptive SOS, offline relay, incident fusion, AI triage (advisory only), privacy-first location, verified responder, live "Where is my help?".

## 2-minute demo script
Click "Auto-play demo" (top bar) and narrate the captions, or run it by hand:
1. Person: choose an ability profile, hold SOS, countdown with cancel.
2. Simulate no internet: SOS hops phone to phone (say it is a simulation).
3. AI triage: severity, confidence, explanation. "Responder rules override AI."
4. Incident fusion: 3 reports become 1 incident.
5. Responder accepts: location goes from "within 300 m" to precise.
6. Live ETA, then Arrived: user sees "Help has arrived".
7. Coordinator: operations view of the whole network.
8. Simple Emergency Mode: accessibility is the foundation, not a feature.

## Slide outline (7 slides)
Problem / Who is excluded today / ResQ in one chain / Live demo / Privacy and AI safety / What is real vs simulated / Roadmap (real mesh, 112 integration, verified responder onboarding).

## Likely judge questions
- Is the offline relay real? No, a labelled simulation of the concept.
- Does AI decide? No, it recommends; responders and rules override.
- Privacy? Only about 300 m precision until a verified responder accepts.
- What is built? FastAPI + Vite prototype, in-memory data, fictional Chennai coordinates.
